## Design System

**Primary color:** #1BB8E8 (Ray Blue)  
**Dark background:** #0d1b2a (Navy) → #1a2e42 → #0a3d5c gradient  
**Accent:** #F4B942 (Gold) — used sparingly on mission strip  
**Typography:** System/inherited fonts, 800 weight for headings  

## Identity

**Tagline:** Education Creates Prosperity  
**Mission strip:** "Ray Foundation helps young people move from education to prosperity"  
**Three Pillars:** Education Access · Leadership & Mentorship · Future Skills & Innovation  
**Signature feature:** The Ray Foundation Pathway to Prosperity (7-step visual model)
